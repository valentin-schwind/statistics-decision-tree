Meaning of 95% Confidence Intervals

A 95% confidence interval shows a plausible range for the population mean. Narrower intervals mean more precision. In repeated sampling, about 95% of such intervals would cover the true mean.

✓ Useful classroom reading: Confidence intervals visualize estimate and uncertainty.

✕ Do not say: “There is a 95% probability that this one interval contains the true mean.”
